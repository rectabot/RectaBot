Safety & Limit Switches

Configure homing, limit switches, and emergency stop behavior.

NPN (sinking) — Recommended

Most inductive sensors (LJ12A3, etc.). Active LOW.

Mechanical switches

Microswitches, optical. NO contact + opto.

RectaBot v1 has individual MIN/HOME switches per axis (X_MIN, Y_MIN, Z_MAX, A_MIN, B_MIN). The DOOR input (CN32) can be repurposed as a serial chain of mechanical MAX switches — recommended for routers without safety door.

🔗 DOOR = MAX switches chain

Wire mechanical NC switches in series on X_MAX, Y_MAX, Z_MIN ends. If any axis hits its end, DOOR opens → grblHAL alarm.

Recommended for routers

🚪 DOOR = Safety door sensor

Cabinet door / enclosure interlock. Pauses motion when door opens. Use when you have a separate MAX protection or only soft limits.

Mill / enclosed machine

⚠️

Soft limits are CRITICAL

Without MAX limit switches (DOOR chain) OR soft limits, your machine has zero protection against running off an axis end (crash, motor stall, damage). Soft limits use the working area from Step 2 as the alarm boundary. We recommend enabling both when possible.
